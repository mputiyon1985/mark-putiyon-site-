AI security

CyberForce

A unified view of defensive and offensive security findings.

The problem

Security teams use different tools for monitoring, vulnerability assessment, and authorized testing. When the same issue appears in several tools, teams need to reconcile the findings before they can act on them.

My role and what shipped

I was the sole developer of the platform. CyberForce combines 14 security tools and 14 specialist AI agents to correlate findings and reduce duplicate reporting. A local model supports report summaries.

The result

A single workflow from engagement scoping through reporting, packaged as a monthly managed service on a compact appliance.
