AI in operations

HaloPSA ticket quality

An AI quality layer for the Technology Innovation Partners service desk.

The problem

Service desk leaders need visibility into ticket quality as well as ticket volume. Evaluating closed tickets consistently is difficult to do at scale through manual review alone.

My role and what shipped

I built AI-based ticket quality scoring on top of HaloPSA and leadership dashboards for reviewing service delivery and operational performance.

Measured use

7,992 closed tickets scored in the published project snapshot.

The service desk separately handled 1,318 tickets in a reported 90-day period, with 79.7% closed at the first tier and 79.1% resolved within the SLA. These figures describe desk performance for that period; they do not measure the effect of the AI scoring layer.
