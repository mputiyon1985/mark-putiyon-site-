Software delivery

CPApex

A live platform that brings the daily work of CPA practices into one place.

The problem

CPA firms can end up tracking clients, hours, invoices, deadlines and documents in separate tools. That splits information across systems and creates repeat entry.

My role and what shipped

I led development with one additional developer. CPApex gives each firm its own tenant and connects workflows through a shared client record. For example, hours logged against a client can flow into an invoice, while deadlines and documents remain tied to the same client.

The result

One live platform designed to replace six or more disconnected tools. It covers client management, time tracking, billing, payments, deadlines and secure document exchange.
