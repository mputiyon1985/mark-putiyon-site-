CPApex

Practice management platform for CPA firms · v3.2 · live at cpapex.net

Lead developer: Mark Putiyon (with one additional developer)

CPApex replaces the stack of disconnected tools a CPA firm runs on (CRM, time tracking, invoicing, payments, document exchange, client portal, tax deadlines, messaging and firm admin) with one multi-tenant platform. Every firm is isolated in its own tenant with its own branded sign-in, and the modules share one client record, so hours logged in TimeTrack become invoice lines, invoices become payments, and deadlines and documents hang off the same client.

10core modules
143API routes
38app screens
56automated test files (unit + Playwright E2E)

Screenshots

Demo firm with seeded sample data; firm and staff names blurred. Click any screen to enlarge.

Product site: one platform in place of 6+ disconnected tools

Modules

  • ClientHub: the firm's CRM. Business and individual clients, prospects and leads, contacts, employees, engagement type, notes, activity and documents on one record, with import from QuickBooks Online.
  • TimeTrack: staff log time against clients and services at per-staff bill rates; billable, non-billable and unbilled hours flow into invoices without re-entry.
  • InvoiceDesk: builds invoices from unbilled time, then moves them through a staged workflow (Pre-Bill, review, invoice prep, ready to send, sent, completed) with batch build and send, invoice notes with edit history, overdue tracking and branded PDFs.
  • Payments: online invoice payment through BluePay (Fiserv) hosted payment forms and CPACharge, so card data never touches CPApex.
  • Deadline-IQ: a federal and Virginia tax and filing calendar (1040-ES, 1065, 1120, 1120-S, 990 and more) with IRS calendar import, batch assignment and overdue / 7-day / 30-day views.
  • SecureDocs: a per-client document store for secure file exchange, replacing email attachments.
  • ClientConnect: the client portal, where clients sign in separately to see their documents, invoices and deadlines.
  • EngageDocs: engagement and fee letters sent to clients for electronic signature.
  • Connect-IQ / FirmPulse: client messaging plus in-app alerts for firm events such as new invoices, messages and portal activity.
  • FirmHQ: firm administration covering users, roles and permissions, locations, branding, services, deadline types, integrations and the audit log.

Security and compliance

  • Multi-factor sign-in with TOTP authenticator apps; firm admins can require it for every staff account, and MFA secrets are stored encrypted
  • Client EIN and SSN fields, QuickBooks tokens and MFA secrets encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM
  • Role-based access with per-staff permissions (for example, who can see bill rates) and strict tenant isolation between firms
  • Audit log of user and system actions, filterable and exportable to CSV for owners and admins
  • Password hashing (bcrypt), per-email and per-IP login lockout, rate limiting, session timeout and re-authentication before sensitive invoice edits
  • Content-Security-Policy headers and HTML sanitization (DOMPurify) on user content
  • Designed around GLBA and IRS Publication 4557 safeguards for tax-preparer data, with SOC 2-minded controls

Integrations

QuickBooks Online (OAuth client and invoice sync) · BluePay by Fiserv · CPACharge · IRS deadline calendar · Resend / SMTP email · Vercel Blob storage

Engineering

Production SaaS on Vercel with separate dev and production databases, pull-request QA on preview deployments, a unit test suite (node:test / tsx) and Playwright end-to-end specs covering auth, CRM, files, invoicing, timecards, portal and admin, plus a written code review (Oct 2026).

PestPilot

Chemical request and inventory app for pest control · in design · clickable mockups at demo.pestpilot.net

Sole developer: Mark Putiyon

PestPilot replaces a paper chemical request form for a pest-control company. Technicians ask for the chemicals and baits they need from a company iPhone, a manager approves each line, and the warehouse pulls the stock and hands it out to each truck. The app keeps its own inventory ledger for the warehouse and every truck, and is designed to work alongside PestPac, the company’s field-service system, by pulling chemical usage from PestPac work orders. The weekly flow is simple: technicians request on Thursday, the manager reviews, the warehouse picks on Friday and hands out on Monday.

Status: the screens are clickable design mockups with made-up sample data, and the production app at pestpilot.net is in its first build stage.

52clickable mock screens (iPhone and desktop)
5user roles with a permission matrix
5steps in the weekly request flow
2modules: chemical inventory and equipment

Screenshots

From the mockups at demo.pestpilot.net. All products, EPA numbers, people, trucks and work orders are fictional sample data. Click any screen to enlarge.

Features

  • Technician iPhone app: PIN or Face ID sign-in, my-truck stock, a picture catalog with EPA number and SDS, a weekly request cart and request status
  • Manager approval queue: approve, reduce or deny each line, with a weekly estimated order by supplier
  • Warehouse: receive stock with PO, lot and expiry; cycle counts with a required reason; a low-stock reorder report; a Friday pick list and a signed Monday handout
  • Trucks: fleet overview, in-service / out-of-service status, standard load (par) levels, driver assignment, truck counts and a ledger of every transfer
  • Equipment module: tagged equipment registry, check-out and return by phone or desktop, overdue and service tracking, repair work orders
  • Safety: an SDS library with all 16 GHS sections, a large-text SDS quick view on the phone, SDS for everything on a truck, and spill / waste / damage reports
  • Control and reports: PestPac usage compared against the ledger, exceptions and duplicates, current stock levels and inventory value

Security and controls (as designed)

  • Five roles (Technician, Certified Applicator, Driver, Warehouse, Approver / Admin) set by a permission matrix
  • Restricted-use chemicals are tied to a person’s valid applicator certification and switch off automatically when it expires
  • A truck holding restricted-use stock cannot be assigned to an uncertified driver
  • Stock changes are recorded as ledger entries with a reason, and spill or damage reports are approved before they post

Tech stack

Mockups: hand-built HTML and CSS, hosted on Vercel. App: Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, libSQL / Turso, Vercel
